# PPP 4x KT88 – Variant Notes

Branch `kt88`: the 6x EL34 circlotron rebuilt with 4x KT88 (2 per bank).
All figures are estimates from data sheets and the assumptions in
`IMPROVEMENTS.md` (≈ 438 V DC rails, Rkk 330 Ω). Check them on the built amplifier.

## Verdict

The swap is feasible and makes sense. Two KT88 per bank replace three EL34 at about
the same total plate dissipation. You need fewer tubes, sockets, bias trimmers and
less heater current, and the KT88 ratings fit this circuit better than the EL34 ratings:

| | 3x EL34 per bank | 2x KT88 per bank |
|---|---|---|
| Plate dissipation (max) | 3 × 25 W = 75 W | 2 × 35 W (GEC) … 42 W (current production) = 70–84 W |
| Screen voltage, Vg2 max | 425 V – exceeded at high mains | 600 V – ≈ 418 V is no problem |
| Heater–cathode, Vhk max | 100 V – exceeded (M1/M2 swing ±150 V) | typ. 200 V (check your brand) – within rating |
| Heater current, total incl. V1/V2 | 9.6 A of 10 A | 7.0 A of 10 A |
| Idle (suggested) | 40 mA ≈ 17 W (70 %) | 50–60 mA ≈ 22–26 W (55–65 % of 42 W) |
| Idle current per HV winding | ≈ 120 mA + screens | ≈ 110 mA + screens – unchanged |

The pinout is compatible (same octal socket). KT88 pin 1 is not connected; the beam
plates are tied internally to the cathode (pin 8).

## Changes in the schematic (this branch)

- V3–V6: new `ppp:KT88` symbol (beam tetrode, no G3 pin). The G3–cathode links are removed.
- Removed the third column: V7, V8, C13, C14, R23, R24, R29, R30, R35, R36, R41, R42,
  R47, R48, P5, P6. Gaps in the reference numbers are intentional: the remaining
  parts keep the same references as in the EL34 version.
- R19–R22 grid leaks: 220k → 150k. With the bias pot in series, 220k sits at or
  above the fixed-bias Rg1 max of most KT88 data sheets (6550: as low as 50k–100k).
  2 × 150k per bank gives the driver almost the same load as 3 × 220k did (75k vs 73k).
- Titles and notes updated. Power supply unchanged.

## Points to check / decide

1. **Output transformer load.** With the AP-634/2 at 330 Ω each tube pair sees
   ≈ 2.6 kΩ (EL34 version: ≈ 4.0 kΩ) and each KT88 must deliver ≈ 1.5× the
   peak current of one EL34 (≈ 0.46 A vs 0.31 A at 140 W). In a circlotron the
   screen supply drops along with the anode, so peak current is limited at low
   Vg2k. Expect the same or slightly lower full power (rough estimate 110–140 W)
   and verify on the bench. Two options:
   - Keep 330 Ω: simplest, and the KT88 can supply the current.
   - 8 Ω speaker on the 6 Ω tap → Rkk ≈ 440 Ω (≈ 3.5 kΩ per pair):
     less current per tube and lower distortion, but a lower power ceiling
     (≈ 100–120 W) and a higher source impedance.
   - Or ask EXPERIENCE electronics about a ≈ 500 Ω Rkk version of the AP-634.
2. **Bias range.** With only two trimmer chains, the bias rail is loaded less:
   ≈ −69 V instead of ≈ −63 V (C15 ≈ −85 V, R17 6.8k). The range becomes
   ≈ −39 V … −69 V, which covers the expected KT88 operating point
   (≈ −45 … −55 V for 50–60 mA at Vg2 ≈ 418 V). R25–R28 stay 33k. Still apply
   the fail-safe wiper change from `IMPROVEMENTS.md` §2.5: at −39 V a KT88
   draws far more than its idle current.
3. **Bias measurement.** R37–R40 10R: 55 mA → 0.55 V. At ≈ 0.46 A peak
   cathode current the dissipation is ≈ 0.5 W at sustained full power, so
   1 W / 1 % is still the right choice.
4. **Driver.** The grid drive is similar to the EL34 version (bias ≈ −50 V instead
   of ≈ −38 V, but the swing is dominated by the ±150 V cathode swing). Check
   that V2 swings cleanly with headroom before clipping.
5. **Tube choice.** Use matched quads from one production lot. Check the brand's
   Pa, Vhk and Rg1 limits: they differ between Genalex/Gold Lion, Tung-Sol, JJ and
   Shuguang/PSVane. 6550 types fit the sockets but have a lower Rg1 max and
   Pa ≈ 35–42 W.
